# TF Switcher supporters

Thank you to everyone who helps maintain and improve TF Switcher.

[Support TF Switcher on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/345dave)

TF Switcher is free to use, with all features available without contributing.
Optional contributions help fund maintenance, testing, and improvements.

## Choose whether to appear here

Public recognition is optional. If you would like to be thanked on this page,
include this in your Ko-fi contribution message, replacing the bracketed text:

> Please list me as [your chosen name or GitHub handle] on the TF Switcher supporters page.

Acknowledgements are added manually after permission is given. Only the name or
handle you approve will be published; contribution amounts and payment details
are kept private. You are equally welcome to contribute without being listed.

To change or remove an acknowledgement, contact [345dave on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/345dave).
